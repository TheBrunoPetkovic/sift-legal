Sift: AI Photo Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Sift: AI Photo Cleaner ("Sift", "we") is built so that your photos stay on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Your photos

With your permission, Sift reads your photo library to find similar photos, blurry shots, screenshots, documents and large videos. All analysis happens on your device using Apple frameworks (such as Vision and on-device Apple Intelligence). Photos, videos, thumbnails and analysis results are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Sift stores a small analysis cache (for example, image fingerprints and sharpness scores) inside the app on your device so later scans are faster. It is deleted when you delete the app, and you can reset it in Settings › Reset and Rescan.

Deleting photos

Sift never deletes anything without your confirmation. Deletions go through the iOS system dialog and items move to Recently Deleted, where they stay for 30 days.

Data we collect

None. Sift has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may share anonymized sales information with us as part of standard App Store reporting.

Children

Sift does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
